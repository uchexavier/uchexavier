### 1. Real Estate Lead Automation System

An AI-powered lead management workflow built with n8n.

- Captures property enquiries through a form.
- Extracts structured information using AI.
- Classifies leads by intent and urgency.
- Stores and updates lead records in Google Sheets.
- Sends WhatsApp notifications to the real estate team.

**Technologies:** n8n, AI integration, Google Sheets, WhatsApp Business Cloud API, Webhooks.

### 2. Automated Order Classification & High-Value Alert System

An order-processing workflow that categorizes incoming orders based on their monetary value.

- Captures order information through a webhook.
- Normalizes monetary values using JavaScript.
- Separates high-value orders from regular orders.
- Stores orders in separate Google Sheets records.
- Sends email alerts for orders above ₦50,000.

**Technologies:** n8n, JavaScript, Google Sheets, Gmail, Webhooks.
